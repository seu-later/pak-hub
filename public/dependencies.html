Dependencies

These are the usual first installs for modded difficulty lobbies.

  1. Custom Difficulty 2 (CD2)

    The framework used by many custom difficulty packs.

    Discord source

  2. More Enemy Variants (MEV)

    Adds the enemy variants commonly referenced by difficulty packs.

    Discord source

  3. Donnie’s Enemy Addon (DEA)

    An enemy expansion used by several custom difficulty setups.

    Open mod.io

  4. Elytra’s Enemies Expansion (EEE)

    A broader enemy roster dependency for modded missions.

    Open mod.io

  5. Enn’s Biome Schmorp

    Biome content that frequently accompanies custom difficulty lobbies.

    Discord source
